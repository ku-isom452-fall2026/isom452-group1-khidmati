About Khidmati

Home services in Kuwait, made simple, transparent and trusted.

Our Mission

Khidmati (خدمتي, meaning "my service") connects residents of Kuwait with reliable home service professionals. We want booking a technician to be as easy and predictable as ordering food online.

Most people today find technicians through word of mouth, WhatsApp groups or social media, with no way to compare prices or check quality. In the summer, when an AC breaks down, that search becomes stressful and urgent. Khidmati brings verified providers, clear prices in KWD and real customer reviews into one place.

What We Stand For

Trust

Every provider is checked by our team using their Civil ID and trade documents before they appear on the platform.

Transparency

Prices are shown in Kuwaiti dinar before you book, so there are no surprises when the job is done.

Accessibility

Designed for Arabic and English speakers, and for every device from phone to laptop.

Fair opportunity

Independent technicians and small companies get a steady stream of customers and a rating they can build on.
