Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

You can pay online with K-Net or pay cash to the provider after the job is finished.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. You can cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled visit from your dashboard.

How are providers verified?

Our team checks each provider's Civil ID and trade documents before approving the account.

What if something goes wrong?

Send us a message using the form and include your booking number. We will review the complaint and contact both sides.
